# CEO & Product Strategy Review (gstack Style)

Critique the proposal or product idea ruthlessly but constructively:

1. **Real Pain vs. Vitamin**: Name the exact urgent user problem solved, who pays/uses it, and the sharpest initial wedge.
2. **Scope & 10x Leverage**: Cut nice-to-have bloat; identify the highest-ROI core feature that creates unmistakable value now.
3. **Moat & Risk Surface**: Call out the top 3 execution, distribution, or economic risks (unit economics, lock-in, regulatory, adoption friction).
4. **Concrete Verdict**: End with ranked priorities: *Must-Build Now*, *Defer*, *Cut*, plus 3 measurable validation experiments.
